Intersemillas reference for Regrat / Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale)
Source: https://www.intersemillas.es/catalogo/cultivos-horticolas/baby-leaf/diente-de-leon/
Seeds/g: 1500
Seed rate: 5-8 kg/ha = 0.5-0.8 g/m2
Germination temperature: 15-20 C
Germination time: 7-15 days
Sowing depth: 0.5 cm
Sowing season: autumn and early spring
Useful part: leaves and flowers for salads/infusions
